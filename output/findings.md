# Five Findings

1. The link between GDP and life expectancy was 0.62 in 2023.

2. Countries with lower GDP had an average life expectancy of 65.0 years.

3. Countries with higher GDP had an average life expectancy of 81.3 years.

4. Median life expectancy changed from 69.1 years in 2000 to 73.8 years in 2023.

5. Monaco had the highest GDP per person in 2023: $256,800.

# Limitations

- Some countries have missing data.
- GDP is measured in US dollars and changes with prices.
- GDP does not show how money is shared among people.
- A link between two things does not prove that one causes the other.
- Health and education can also affect life expectancy.
